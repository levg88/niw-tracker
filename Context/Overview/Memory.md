## Facts
- NIW Evidence Tracker is a local-first web app for a self-filing EB-2 National Interest Waiver exceptional-ability case; it turns the regulation and the Dhanasar test into a checklist of evidence items, tracks uploaded documents, scores progress, and produces a printable readiness report. (General)
- It is informational only: the app does not predict approval, determine legal sufficiency, or replace an immigration attorney. (General)
- The catalog models four layers: identity/procedural items, the seven exceptional-ability criteria A–G (at least three must be documented), the three Dhanasar prongs, and supporting materials. (General)
- Each evidence item carries a description, legal citation, checklist criteria, improvement tips, required/optional status, a 1–5 weight, and upload slots; the catalog contains roughly 39 items. (General)
- The codebase is TypeScript on Node.js 20+, Express 5, EJS views, SQLite via better-sqlite3, and multer for uploads. (General)
- Top-level layout: `src/` application source (`app.ts`, `server.ts`, `routes.ts`, `catalog.ts`, `scoring.ts`, `db.ts`, `config.ts`, `types.ts`), `views/` EJS templates, `public/` static assets, `tests/` Vitest suites. (General)
- Runtime data lives under `data/` (ignored by git): `niw.db` and `uploads/`. (General)
- Configuration is environment-only (`NIW_HOST`, `NIW_PORT`, `NIW_DATA_DIR`, `NIW_MAX_UPLOAD_MB`); there is no config file and no authentication. (General)
- The project is MIT licensed. (General)

## Gotchas
- The static catalog is re-synced into SQLite on every start: item text, criteria, and tips update, but progress, notes, overrides, and documents are left untouched. (General)
- Overall readiness is not a plain average: it weights every required item plus the applicant's three strongest exceptional-ability criteria, and optional items do not affect the headline score. (General)
- A zero score with no documents is "Missing"; an unscored item with at least one document is "Needs improvement", not "Missing". (General)
- Keep the bind address on loopback. Without authentication, exposing the app to the internet exposes the case file and uploads. (General)
- `data/` is gitignored and the database and uploads are plain files: back them up separately, and use full-disk encryption. (General)
- Legal content ages: verify current USCIS forms, fees, and policy before relying on any citation in the catalog. (General)
